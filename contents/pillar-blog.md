## Tips to Maintain Great Oral Health Between Visits

Even the best dental clinic can't replace good daily habits. Here's what our dentists at SmileCare consistently recommend:

* Brush twice daily using a soft-bristled toothbrush and fluoride toothpaste
* Floss daily to remove plaque between teeth where brushes can't reach
* Limit sugary snacks and beverages, especially between meals
* Stay hydrated to support saliva production, which naturally protects teeth
* Schedule a professional cleaning every six months, even if nothing feels wrong

**Small, consistent habits often prevent the big, expensive dental problems down the line.**
